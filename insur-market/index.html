GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might ask.

This is a concept app, so the answers explain how this demo behaves.

Is this real insurance?+

No. Coverly is a fictional product demo. Prices and coverage examples are illustrative, and saving a plan does not create insurance coverage.

Will I be charged?+

No. This demo has no checkout, payment processing, or billing.

Where are my demo plans saved?+

Plans and sample claims are saved only in this browser's local storage. Clearing browser data removes them.

Can I make a real claim here?+

No. The claim form demonstrates an interface only. It does not send information to an insurer or start a real claim.
